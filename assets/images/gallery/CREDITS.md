# Gallery placeholder images

Stand-ins for the photographs people will submit through the form. They come
from Wikimedia Commons under the licences listed below, and they are here to
show the gallery working - not to ship.

Two things to settle before launch:

- **Attribution.** Everything marked CC BY or CC BY-SA has to carry its credit
  wherever it is published. Nothing on the page shows one yet, so either add
  visible credits or replace the images.
- **Editorial fit.** These were picked for subject and legibility, not by
  Amnistia. A couple are approximate - the reproductive-rights tile is a
  general women's-rights march, for instance. Photographs centred on one
  identifiable person were avoided on purpose: someone photographed at one
  protest should not end up captioned with a cause they did not put their
  name to.

Each file is centre-cropped to its tile's aspect ratio and capped at 1100px on
the long edge.
